# NAIL DIARY 💅

A small nail-biting tracker: switch each day to red (bitten) or green (not bitten),
add a left- and right-hand photo, and watch the month and year fill in.

Live: https://mcstrunck.github.io/nail-diary/

## How data is stored on GitHub Pages
Everything is saved **on the device you use**: red/green days in localStorage,
photos in IndexedDB (resized to 1200px). That's room for well over 730 photos
(a year at 2 a day is roughly 150–250 MB). There is no sync between devices.

Add the app to your Home Screen: Safari can clear website data for sites you
haven't opened in a while, but Home Screen apps keep their storage.
